06 // How to Apply & Fast-Track

SBIR Application Workflow & Fast-Track Tips

Four clear steps to submit cleanly—and three proven ways to cut months off approval.

4-Step Workflow

Start Paperwork 4–6 Weeks Early

01Mandatory Registrations
02Topic Alignment
03Technical Proposal
04Commercial Strategy
Step 01 Do First

Mandatory Registrations

Complete SAM.gov (UEI), SBC Registry, and Grants.gov / eRA Commons before drafting—account approvals take 2–4 weeks.

Step 02 Match Mission

Topic Alignment

Map our AEO technology directly to open agency solicitations (FOAs / BAAs) using the agency's exact mission language.

Step 03 Prove Innovation

Technical Proposal

Present a focused research plan built around clear innovation, realistic milestones, and measurable results—keep the summary plain English.

Step 04 Show Commercial Pull

Commercial Strategy

Show market size, IP protection, competitive edge, and real customer traction (including letters of support from private-sector buyers).

Fast-Tracking SBIR Approvals

01

Target "Fast-Track"

Combine Phase I & II in one submission (NIH / DoW) to eliminate the standard 6-month funding gap.

02

Engage Program Officers

Send a 1-page concept paper 4–6 weeks before release—direct alignment sharply raises proposal scores.

03

Pre-Submission Audit

Submit 5 days early to clear automated portal checks—tiny formatting errors cause instant rejection.

Tip 01 // Support Letters

Attach 2–3 letters from real commercial or agency buyers.

Tip 02 // Mirror the Rubric

Match section headings directly to the scoring checklist.

Tip 03 // Tight Milestones

Tie every Phase I dollar to one clear, provable goal.

Tip 04 // Use Debriefs

Always request reviewer notes—resubmissions win far more often.

07 // SBA Set-Asides (Solo Track)

Winning Federal Contracts Without Competing Against Giants.

Federal law reserves at least 23% of government contract dollars for small businesses—locking multi-billion-dollar defense contractors out of bidding directly against us.

Automatic Set-Aside
Under ~$250K–$350K

Smaller contracts are automatically reserved for small businesses—faster awards and ideal first wins.

The "Rule of Two"
Multi-Million Deals

If at least two small businesses can fulfill a larger contract at a fair price, agencies restrict it to small firms.

Competition Radar
Shrinking the Bidder Pool

Track A: Winning Set-Asides On Our Own (How to Cut Competition)

5 Tactical Moves
01 // Find Deals Early

SAM.gov & Expiring Contracts

Track Small Business Set-Aside alerts on SAM.gov and spot expiring contracts 6–12 months early on USAspending.gov.

02 // Shape the Bid

Answer Early Market Notices

Respond to "Sources Sought" notices before bids open to trigger the Rule of Two and shape requirements around AEO.

03 // Avoid Crowded Bids

Own the AI & Cyber Niche

Skip generic IT bids with 80+ competitors. Target AI defense and search security where legacy contractors have zero AEO skill.

04 // Build Track Record Fast

Start With $50K–$250K Pilots

Close rapid, low-competition audits and pilots first to earn the federal past-performance ratings bigger deals require.

05 // Direct Introductions

Partner With Agency Advocates

Meet each agency's Small Business Office with a 1-page Capability Statement—they are graded on helping small firms win.
