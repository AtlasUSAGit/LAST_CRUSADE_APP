06 // Application Playbook & Fast-Track

Step-by-Step SBIR Application Guide & Fast-Track Playbook

Most first-time applicants fail on administrative friction, not technology. Follow this four-step sequence and three proven accelerators to move cleanly from registration to funded award.

SBIR Application Workflow

Four Disciplined Steps Before Submission Day

Start mandatory registrations at least 4 to 6 weeks before your target deadline so paperwork never blocks a winning proposal.

01Mandatory Registrations
02Topic Alignment
03Technical Proposal
04Commercial Strategy
Step 01 // Foundation Weeks 1–3

Mandatory Registrations

Complete SAM.gov (UEI), the SBA Small Business Concern (SBC) Registry, and Grants.gov / eRA Commons (plus the DSIP portal for DoW) prior to drafting.

Pro Tip: SAM.gov entity validation can take 2–4 weeks if address or EIN records don't match IRS filings character-for-character. Register immediately—it is 100% free and opens the door to both SBIR and SBA Set-Asides.
Step 02 // Targeting Weeks 3–4

Topic Alignment

Map our AEO & Counter-AEO technology directly to active agency Funding Opportunity Announcements (FOAs), Broad Agency Announcements (BAAs), or DoW Open Topics.

Pro Tip: Never force a generic pitch into an unrelated solicitation. Mirror the exact mission language of the agency—e.g., "information integrity & cyber resilience" for DHS/DoW, or "trustworthy AI standards" for NIST/DOC.
Step 03 // Blueprint Weeks 4–7

Technical Proposal

Draft a rigorous, crystal-clear research plan emphasizing technical innovation, measurable milestones, clear risk reduction, and an executable delivery schedule.

Pro Tip: Reviewers read dozens of dense proposals quickly. Open with a plain-English executive summary explaining the threat, why existing security tools miss AI retrieval manipulation, and the exact measurable outcome our prototype will prove.
Step 04 // Scale Case Weeks 6–8

Commercial Strategy

Provide credible market sizing, our IP defense strategy, competitor analysis, and real customer traction metrics (including private-sector AEO engagements and letters of support).

Pro Tip: Agencies don't want science projects that sit on a shelf—they want technologies that survive commercially. Showing dual-use commercial pull (such as enterprise AEO work with AWS and financial firms) puts our commercialization score in the top tier.

Fast-Tracking SBIR Approvals

01

Target "Fast-Track"

Turn Cold Submissions Into Warm Advocacy

Pre-Submission Audit

Submit at least 5 days early to clear SAM.gov and Grants.gov automated validation checks. Minor page-count, margin, PDF font, or budget-table glitches cause immediate administrative rejection without ever being read.

Attach 2–4 signed letters from commercial customers (or federal end-users) stating they will adopt the finished tool.

Structure proposal headings to mirror the exact evaluation criteria in the solicitation so reviewers can award full points easily.

Tip C // Right-Size the Ask

Budget every dollar to concrete milestones—avoid padded overhead and keep Phase I laser-focused on proving one undeniable metric.

Tip D // Request Debriefs

If an application isn't funded on the first try, always request written reviewer feedback—resubmissions win at dramatically higher rates.

07 // SBA Small Business Set-Asides (Solo Track)

Winning Federal Contracts Without Competing Against Defense Giants.

The U.S. government is required by federal policy to reserve a major share of its contract spending—at least 23% of prime federal contracting dollars—for small businesses. Through SBA Small Business Set-Asides, agencies legally restrict specific contracts so multi-billion-dollar primes cannot bid directly against us.

Automatic Reservation
Simplified Acquisitions

Contracts between $10,000 and the Simplified Acquisition Threshold (~$250K–$350K) are automatically set aside for small businesses—faster paperwork, rapid awards, and ideal for building early federal past performance.

The "Rule of Two"
Multi-Million Set-Asides

Above the threshold, if a Contracting Officer expects at least two capable small businesses to submit fair-priced bids, the contract is reserved exclusively for small businesses—locking out huge primes.

Track A // Winning Small Business Set-Asides On Our Own

Tactical Guide: Finding Contracts & Engineering Low-Competition Wins

Solo Execution Playbook
01 // Where to Hunt Daily

SAM.gov & Agency Forecasts

Set saved alerts on SAM.gov filtered strictly to Total Small Business Set-Aside under AI, cybersecurity, and data NAICS codes (e.g., 541511, 541512, 541715). Cross-check USAspending.gov to spot expiring contracts 6–12 months before renewal.

02 // Shape the Bid Early

Answer Sources Sought & RFIs

Before posting an RFP, agencies publish Sources Sought Notices to see if small businesses can do the job. Responding with a sharp capability brief proves the Rule of Two, nudges the buyer to set the contract aside, and shapes the technical specs around AEO.

03 // Exploit Our Category Edge

Target Emerging AI & Cyber Niches

Avoid generic IT helpdesk or broad staffing bids where 80+ small shops compete on lowest price. Target generative AI defense, misinformation detection, retrieval security, and OSINT analytics—where virtually no traditional Beltway contractor has AEO expertise.

04 // Fast First Wins

Start Under the Simplified Threshold

Target $50K–$250K pilot audits, AI vulnerability assessments, and rapid-prototyping awards first. These face minimal competition, close in weeks instead of years, and generate the federal Past Performance (CPARS) scores needed for multi-million-dollar bids.

05 // Direct Buyer Access

Meet Agency Small Business Offices

Every major department has an OSDBU (Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization) graded on hitting small-business spending quotas. Bring a crisp 1-page Capability Statement focused on AI security gaps—they actively introduce small firms to buyers.

06 // The Solo Limit (And How We Break It)

Why Solo Set-Asides Have a Ceiling

On our own, we can rapidly win pilots, SBIR grants, and niche contracts up to ~$1M–$5M. To capture $10M–$100M+ federal Small Business Set-Asides that demand deep past performance, GovCloud hosting, and security clearances, we unlock Track B: The SBA Mentor-Protégé Program with AWS.
